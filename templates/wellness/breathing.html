Box Breathing Technique

5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding

Focus on your immediate environment to anchor your mind during times of high anxiety or overwhelming emotions.

Look around your room. Notice details: a pen on the desk, light reflecting on a frame, a shadow, a pattern on the wall, or a plant leaf.

Feel the texture of your shirt, the firmness of your chair, the cool surface of your table, or the ground beneath your feet.

Listen closely: a distant car, the whirring of a fan, birds chirping, or your own calm breath.

Notice smells around you: fresh air, coffee, paper, soap, or essential oils.

Notice the taste in your mouth, or take a sip of water or a mint.
