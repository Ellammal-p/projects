When to Consult a Mental Health Professional

It is recommended to seek guidance from a licensed psychiatrist, clinical psychologist, or professional counselor if you experience:

  • Persistent sadness, emptiness, or feeling overwhelmed for more than 2 weeks
  • Significant disruption in daily sleeping patterns or appetite
  • Loss of interest in activities that previously brought joy
  • Difficulty concentrating on college coursework or daily routine tasks
  • Withdrawal from family, social connections, and peer activities
  • Feeling hopeless or struggling with persistent anxious thoughts
